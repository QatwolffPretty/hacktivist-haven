> FAQ05 / HELP
Who can join Hacktivist Haven?

We have tracks for adults, IT students and kids. Previous cybersecurity experience is not required for beginner-level learning.

Are the sessions practical?

Yes. The programme is designed around instructor guidance, exercises, labs, scenarios and structured progression.

Is ethical hacking taught legally?

Training should be performed only in authorised, controlled environments. We focus on responsible learning and safe practice.

How do I register?

Contact Hacktivist Haven through Instagram or your chosen community channel so the team can confirm the next available class and payment instructions.

Do you offer trial sessions?

Yes. Trial pricing is RM48 for Adult, RM29 for IT Student and RM20 for Kids.
